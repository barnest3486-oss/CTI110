# Elena Ruz Sandwich

## Assumed Positions
-Medianoche bread roll is on the robot's left
-melted butter is infront of the creme cheese
-Creme cheese is next to the bread
-Guava Jam is behind the softened creme cheese
-3 Thinly sliced roast Turkey breast is next to the bread as well
-Knife to spread jams and creme cheese is in the robot's right hand
-everything is on a cutting board that's on a marble kitchen counter

## Instructions
1. Slice the roll in half lengthwise & brush the inside surfaces lightly with the butter
2. Place the split roll face-down on a skillet or griddle over medium heat & toast for 1 to 2 minutes until the inside is lightly golden and warm
3. Remove the roll from the heat, spread the cream cheese evenly onto the bottom half of the bread & then spread the strawberry jam generously onto the top half
4. Fold the roast turkey slices neatly over the cream cheese on the bottom half
5. Close the sandwich, cut it diagonally in half, and serve immediately while the bread is warm

## Done When
- The sandwich has been served
